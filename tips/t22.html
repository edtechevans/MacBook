MacBook · HOW TO

Capture or record your screen

A quick, practical reference you can use right away.

START HERE · FAST PATH
⇧ ⌘ 5
⎙ Print

How to do it

Step 1

Press Shift + Command + 5 for the Screenshot toolbar

Step 2

Choose a screenshot or recording mode; set microphone and destination in Options

Step 3

Start recording and use the menu-bar stop button to finish

EXAMPLE / WHEN THIS HELPS

Record a short, non-sensitive demo.

Good to know

Avoid recording student information or confidential emails.
