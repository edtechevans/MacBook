Teams · HOW TO

Schedule a Teams meeting

A quick, practical reference you can use right away.

START HERE · FAST PATH
Teams → Calendar → New
⎙ Print

How to do it

Step 1

Open Teams Calendar and select New or a time slot

Step 2

Add title, attendees, date and time; use scheduling support if needed

Step 3

Enable Teams meeting for an online meeting and select Save

EXAMPLE / WHEN THIS HELPS

Find the online-meeting toggle, then cancel your practice event.

Good to know

Do not accidentally invite real people when practising.
